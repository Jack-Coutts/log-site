How it works

From the gym to your stats in seconds.

  1. Log the session

    Tap what you did and type the minutes. That's it.

  2. Watch your year fill in

    Every day is a square. The more you do, the darker it gets.

  3. Look back

    Browse by month, or search every session by type or note.

  4. Know your numbers

    Streaks, breaks, weekly averages and where your time goes.

Sick days and holidays

A week off doesn't break your streak.

Mark a day, or a fortnight, as off sick or on holiday. Days off bridge your streak without adding to it, show up red and blue on your heatmap, and stay out of your averages.

Already use the web Exercise Tracker? Import its CSV and your whole history comes across. Export any time in the same date, exercise_type, duration_minutes, notes format, or share a PDF report of your stats, heatmaps and log.
