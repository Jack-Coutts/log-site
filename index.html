How it works

From the gym to your stats in seconds.

  1. Log the session

    Tap +, pick what you did and type the minutes. It takes seconds.

  2. Watch your year fill in

    Every day is a square, darker the more you do. Sick days, injuries and holidays keep your streak.

  3. Know your numbers

    Streaks, weekly averages, records, and stats for every exercise.

  4. Look back

    Browse every session as a list or a calendar, or search your notes.

Private by design

Your exercise stays on your phone.

No account, nothing online

Open the app and start logging. There's nothing to sign up for and nothing posted anywhere.

No ads, no tracking

log collects nothing. Your sessions and notes are yours alone.

Yours to keep

Export a CSV whenever you like, and bring your history in from the web Exercise Tracker.
