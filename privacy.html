Privacy policy

Last updated 7 October 2026

streak is a private exercise log. It's designed so your data stays yours.

What streak collects

Nothing. streak has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer receives no data about you or how you use the app.

Where your data lives

Your sessions, notes, exercise types and settings are stored only on your device. Your phone's own backup, such as iCloud, may include them.

Sharing

Data leaves your device only when you choose to send it:

  • Share report creates a PDF of your stats, heatmaps and log, including your notes.
  • Export CSV creates a file of every session, including your notes.

In each case your device's share sheet sends the file, and you choose where it goes. Import CSV reads only the file you pick.

Children

streak doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that sends data off your device, such as optional sync, this policy will be updated before that version is released and will say exactly what is sent and why.
